## 🇨🇳 中文

端到端做播客的规划、制作和营销，把播客当增长渠道运营。覆盖概念与形式选择、访谈与独白两类单集结构、按预算匹配的音频质量基线、嘉宾寻找与外联、SEO 优化的 shownote，以及从启动到规模化的增长策略与变现路线图。输入是播客概念或已有节目，输出是完整策略文档（单集结构、10—20 位嘉宾 wishlist 与联络路径、前 8 集启动计划、3 个月与 6 个月增长节奏、变现路线图）、嘉宾外联邮件模板、shownote 模板和单集推广 checklist，并在回复里以可直接复制的代码块再给一遍。纯文本产物，不在内部做任何音频工作；录制、剪辑、TTS、转写与邮件发送会指路到对应工具。适合启动新节目、规划播客策略、跑嘉宾外联、给已有节目做变现。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 新节目完整策略：单集结构 + 10—20 位嘉宾 wishlist 与联络路径 + 前 8 集启动计划
- 访谈与独白两套逐分钟时间轴模板，从 cold open 到 outro 全覆盖
- 音频质量基线：麦克风、环境、录音、托管、后期按最低与推荐两档对标预算
- 嘉宾管理：5 条找嘉宾途径 + 外联邮件模板 + 录制前 checklist
- SEO shownote 模板：标题关键词、Key Takeaways、时间戳、资源链接与订阅入口
- 增长与变现：3/6 个月节奏表 + 变现路线图，另附单集推广 checklist

## 🇬🇧 English

Runs podcast planning, production and marketing end to end, treating the show as a growth channel. It covers concept and format choice, episode structures for both interview and monologue shows, an audio-quality baseline matched to budget, guest sourcing and outreach, SEO-optimised show notes, and growth and monetisation roadmaps from launch to scale. The input is a podcast concept or an existing show, and the output is a complete strategy document — episode structure, a 10–20 name guest wishlist with contact paths, a first-eight-episode launch plan, 3-month and 6-month growth cadence, and a monetisation roadmap — plus a guest outreach email template, a show-notes template and a per-episode promotion checklist, all returned again as ready-to-copy code blocks. Everything is plain text: no audio work happens inside, and recording, editing, TTS, transcription and email sending are handed off to the right tools. Best for launching a show, planning podcast strategy, running guest outreach or monetising an existing programme.

### ✨ What it does

- Full new-show strategy: episode structure, a 10–20 name guest wishlist with contact paths, and a first-eight-episode launch plan
- Two minute-by-minute timeline templates for interview and monologue formats, from cold open to outro
- Audio-quality baseline: microphone, room, recording, hosting and post-production benchmarked at minimum and recommended tiers against budget
- Guest management: five sourcing routes, an outreach email template and a pre-recording checklist
- SEO show-notes template with keyword title, Key Takeaways, timestamps, resource links and subscribe links
- Growth and monetisation: a 3-month and 6-month cadence table plus a monetisation roadmap and per-episode promotion checklist
